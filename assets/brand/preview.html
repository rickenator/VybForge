Rules

Do Keep the V visor and the two eye lamps visible — they are the whole mark. Use the icon on the dark tile for app/favicon duties, and the mascot bare on dark surfaces. Minimum icon size 16 px; minimum mascot width 96 px.
Don't Recolor the visor away from magma amber/orange, rotate the mascot, add a second glow hue, put the transparent mascot on a light background without the dark tile, or let the hammer overlap the face panel.
